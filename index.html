01 — The atelier

We build furniture the way it used to be built — slowly, by hand, out of wood that deserves to be kept.

Twenty-six years ago we opened a small workshop in Tanta with two benches and a borrowed lathe. Today six makers work in the same building, on the same street, with the same rule: nothing leaves until we would live with it ourselves.

Mostafa Shaaban

05 — Inside the atelier

Six pairs of hands. One standard.

Every frame is joined — not screwed — by a single maker who signs the underside when it is finished. Ask us and we will show you the signature.

We season our own timber, mix our own finishes and upholster in-house. It makes us slower than most. It also makes our furniture worth keeping.

06 — How it is made

Three stages.
No shortcuts.

01

Drawn

We start with your room — its light, its proportions, the way you actually sit. The first drawing is usually the quietest one.

02

Joined

Frames are cut from timber we have seasoned ourselves and joined with mortise and tenon. No particle board. No shortcuts under the upholstery.

03

Finished

Hand-sanded through five grits, oiled, then left to rest for a week before it is wrapped. The maker signs it on the day it leaves.

“A room should feel finished the moment you stop noticing the furniture.”

Mostafa Shaaban — founder
