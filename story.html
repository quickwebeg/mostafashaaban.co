Since 1998

A workshop that never learned to hurry.

Twenty-six years, one address in Tanta, and a rule that has not changed: nothing leaves until we would live with it ourselves.

Founded1998
MakersSix
AddressOne
Pieces delivered6,400+

Mostafa Shaaban opened his first bench in 1998 with two chisels, a borrowed lathe and a stubborn belief that furniture should be built to be repaired, not replaced. The workshop was one room on El Geish Street. It is still there.

In the years since, the atelier has grown to six makers and a small drawing room where clients sit while we sketch. What has not changed is the sequence: we season the timber ourselves, we join the frames ourselves, and we sign the underside of every piece before it is wrapped.

We do not hold stock. Everything is made to order, which means everything is made for someone. It also means we can tell you, without checking a system, which tree your table came from and who cut the joint.

The years

A short timeline.

1998
One bench, one room

Mostafa Shaaban opens a single-bench workshop on El Geish Street, repairing furniture for neighbours and building the first Verdant chair for his own home.

2006
The first six makers

The workshop takes on a second room and a team of joiners. The signature on the underside of each frame becomes a rule rather than a habit.

2015
Upholstery comes in-house

We stop sending frames to Cairo. Upholstery moves into the building, along with a small archive of fabrics woven in Faiyum and Mahalla.

2021
The six signatures

Our archive is organised into six styles — classic, modern, contemporary, boho, rustic and industrial — so clients can begin with a language rather than a list.

2026
Still one address

Six thousand four hundred pieces later, we are still on El Geish Street, still making to order, still signing the underside.

What we hold to

Three things we will not compromise.

01

Real timber

Seasoned hardwood, joined properly. No particle board behind the upholstery, no veneer pretending to be a tree.

02

One maker, one piece

A single craftsman follows your piece from first cut to final oil, and signs it. If it ever needs attention, we know exactly whose hands to ask.

03

Repair before replace

We keep patterns from every commission. Ten years from now, if a joint loosens or a cushion flattens, we will fix it rather than sell you another.

“We are not in the business of furniture. We are in the business of years.”
